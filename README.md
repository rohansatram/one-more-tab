# One Tab Closer — Memento Mori

A browser extension that replaces your new tab with a live countdown to your approximate death. Every new tab is a reminder to make your time count.

**[Install for Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/one-tab-closer/)**

## Features

- **Live countdown** — years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining based on a 73-year life expectancy
- **Three themes** — Obsidian (dark grey), Void (pure black), and Bone (light)
- **Expanded stats** — life completion percentage, remaining weekends, mornings, books, trips, and real actuarial death probabilities
- **Minimal and private** — no data leaves your browser, everything is stored locally

## How It Works

1. Install the extension
2. Enter your date of birth
3. Every new tab now shows your countdown

Hit **Show More** to see detailed life stats and mortality probabilities. Cycle through themes with the button in the bottom-right corner.
